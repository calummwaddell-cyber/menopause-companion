Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I make a summary for my doctor?

Open Patterns and tap Doctor PDF. It includes your stage, any family history you've added, 30-day totals and your day-by-day logs. Your private note is never included.

I logged twice today — will it count twice?

No. There's one log per day. Opening Log again lets you edit today's entry.

How do I turn the HRT reminder on or off?

Tap the profile button at the top of Today, then use the Hormone therapy section.

How do I lock the app?

Profile button → Privacy → Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID / passcode).

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Profile button → Your data. Export everything creates a spreadsheet (CSV) of all your logs. Delete all my data permanently erases everything from the app.

Does it work in dark mode or with larger text?

Yes. It follows your iPhone's appearance and text size automatically, and you can override both in the profile screen.

Important

Menopause Companion offers general information to help you understand your symptoms and talk to your clinician. It isn't a diagnosis or a substitute for medical advice. If you're worried about a symptom — especially any bleeding after menopause — please see a doctor. If you're struggling and need to talk to someone now, contact your local emergency services or a free listening service such as Samaritans (116 123, UK & Ireland) or 988 (US & Canada).
