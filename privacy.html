Menopause Companion

Privacy Policy

The short version: everything you log stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

Effective 6 November 2026

What the app stores

Menopause Companion stores the information you choose to enter: your first name (optional), menopause stage, symptoms, hot flashes, sleep, bleeding, mood, notes (including the private note), HRT reminder time, family history and display preferences.

All of this is stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The developer has no access to anything you enter.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party software development kits, and it makes no network requests with your data. The App Store privacy label for this app is “Data Not Collected”.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. If you tap Doctor PDF, Share with your partner or Export everything, iOS shows its standard share sheet and you decide where it goes (for example Mail, Messages or Files). Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

Your private pelvic-health note is never included in the doctor summary or partner message. It is included in Export everything, because that export is your complete personal copy.

Like all iPhone apps, the app's data may be included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have those turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Notifications

If you turn on an HRT reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. Reminders are created and delivered locally; no reminder data is sent to us.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your identity. The app only receives a yes or no answer — it never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Deleting your data

Go to You (the profile button on Today) → Your data → Delete all my data to permanently erase everything the app has stored. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all of its data.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side — you are in full control of it on your device. If you have a question about privacy, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
